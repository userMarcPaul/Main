Chicken Pasta of your Dreams

55 mins
4 servings
Medium
4.7 (128 reviews)
F

Florencio

4.4M followers · Food Creator

For all those times you've just gotta have a creamy pasta! This chicken pasta combines tender, juicy chicken breast with a rich, creamy garlic parmesan sauce that coats every single strand of fettuccine. It's the kind of comfort food that makes you close your eyes and savour every single bite. Ready in under an hour with ingredients you probably already have.

Ingredients

🍗 Chicken Breast
500 g
🍝 Fettuccine Pasta
400 g
🧄 Garlic (minced)
4 cloves
🧀 Parmesan Cheese
1 cup
🥛 Heavy Cream
250 ml
🧈 Butter
2 tbsp
🌿 Fresh Basil
handful
🧂 Salt & Pepper
to taste

Instructions

1

Season the chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and a pinch of paprika. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

2

Cook the chicken for 6–7 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Remove from the pan and let rest, then slice into strips.

3

While the chicken rests, cook the fettuccine in a large pot of salted boiling water according to package directions. Drain, reserving 1 cup of pasta water.

4

In the same skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.

5

Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer. Stir in the parmesan cheese until melted and smooth. Add pasta water as needed for consistency.

6

Toss the cooked pasta in the creamy sauce. Top with sliced chicken and fresh basil. Serve immediately with extra parmesan. Enjoy!
